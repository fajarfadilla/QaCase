# Flow 2 - Safety Hazard Report (Manual)

## Assumptions

The case leaves these behaviours unspecified. The test cases below are written against these assumptions.
| ID | Ambiguity | Assumption | Affects | Question for PO |
|----|-----------|------------|---------|-----------------|
| AS-16 | Step 2 says submission generates "a hazard entry, and a followup task" | One Hazard Report creates exactly one Hazard (status Open) and one Followup Task; the Hazard becomes Resolved when its Followup Task is submitted | HZ-M-001, HZ-M-008 | Can a Hazard have more than one Followup Task? Does it have statuses beyond Open / Resolved? |
| AS-17 | "All people in the area receive notification" doesn't define who is "in the area" | Area Members of the Hazard's Area are notified; the Reporter is excluded; the PIC gets only the Followup Task notification, not a second Hazard notification | HZ-M-007 | Who counts as "people in the area": assigned Employees, people currently on site, or something else? |
| AS-18 | Step 5 says "Direct Supervisor of the area will receive notification for it" without saying what "it" is | The Direct Supervisor of the Hazard's Area is notified when the Followup Task is submitted; if the Area has none, the submission still succeeds | HZ-M-014 | Is the Direct Supervisor notified on Followup Task submission, Hazard creation, or both? What if the Area has no Direct Supervisor? |
| AS-19 | The relationship between the Location, Sublocation and Area selects is not described | They cascade: options are filtered by the parent, and changing a parent clears its children | HZ-M-003 | Confirm the hierarchy and cascading behaviour |
| AS-20 | PIC is "preselect to reporter" but who else can be chosen, and who can submit the Followup Task, is not stated | PIC can be changed to any Employee in the same Tenant; only the PIC can submit the Followup Task | HZ-M-004, HZ-M-013 | Is PIC limited to Area Members or a role? Can anyone else (e.g. the Direct Supervisor) submit or reassign the Followup Task? |
| AS-21 | Hazard Report Evidence is mandatory but no limits are given | At least 1 photo is required; camera and gallery are both allowed; more than one photo can be added; large photos are compressed | HZ-M-002 | How many photos are allowed and what is the size limit? Camera only, or gallery too? |
| AS-22 | Followup Task Evidence is not marked Mandatory or Optional | Followup Task Evidence is optional | HZ-M-011 | Is Evidence required to submit a Followup Task? |
| AS-23 | Resolution Date has no validation rules | Resolution Date can't be in the future or earlier than the time the Hazard was reported | HZ-M-009, HZ-M-010 | What are the valid bounds for Resolution Date? |
| AS-24 | Co Observer allows adding more selects with (+), with no rules | Co Observers are optional; the same Employee can't be added twice; the PIC can't be a Co Observer; extra selects can be removed | HZ-M-008, HZ-M-011, HZ-M-012 | Is there a maximum number of Co Observers? Can the PIC be one? |
| AS-25 | Offline submission is implied by the offline goal but not described for this flow | Hazard Reports and Followup Tasks submitted offline are saved as pending and synced automatically once online; notifications are sent only after sync | HZ-M-005, HZ-M-015 | Should anything be notified before the record reaches the backend (e.g. via SMS)? |
| AS-26 | The Notification Service handles "push notification, emails, webhook" but the channel for each event is not stated | Hazard, Followup Task and Direct Supervisor notifications are push notifications in WeMine; tapping one opens the related Hazard or Followup Task | HZ-M-006, HZ-M-014 | Which channel(s) does each notification use? |

## Test Data

| Name | Role |
|------|------|

| Location `Site North` → Sublocation `Workshop` → Area `Bay 2` | A different Area in the same Location |
| Location `Site South` → Sublocation `Pit 3` → Area `Ramp A` | A different Location |
| Rina | Employee; Reporter; Area Member of `Crusher Zone` |
| Paul | Employee; usual PIC; Area Member of `Crusher Zone` |

| Omar | Employee; Area Member of `Bay 2` only |
